Layout and Binding Instructions:
1. When printing, please select "Double-sided Printing (Flip on long edge)" and set paper to A4 Portrait.
2. After printing, cut the paper along the horizontal dashed lines to create 3 strips.
3. Place the strip containing the "Cover" at the bottom (cover side down), and stack the rest in order.
4. Fold in the middle to bind into a booklet with left pages as PPT slides and right pages as notes!
