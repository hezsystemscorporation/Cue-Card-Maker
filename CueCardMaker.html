Layout and Binding Instructions:
1. When printing, please select "Double-sided Printing (Flip on long edge)" and set the paper size to A4 Portrait.
2. After printing on A4 paper, cut the paper along the horizontal dashed lines to create 3 strips.
3. Place the strip containing the "Cover" at the bottom (cover side down), and stack the remaining strips in print order.
4. Finally, fold in the middle to bind into a small booklet with the left pages as PPT slides and the right pages as speaker notes!
